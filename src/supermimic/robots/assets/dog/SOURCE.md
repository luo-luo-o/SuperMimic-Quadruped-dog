# CAD dog model provenance and corrections

Source: local `rlsar/src/rl_sar_zoo/dog_description`. The generated meshes come from its 13 STL files; `dog.urdf` and `dog.xml` are corrected, self-contained descriptions. Rebuild with `uv run python scripts/robots/port_dog.py --source /path/to/dog_description`.

The CAD coordinate convention is X right, Y rear, Z up. Generated meshes and robot frames use X forward, Y left, Z up. Each STL is transformed through the *full original URDF joint and visual-origin chain* at source zero pose, then expressed in its new link frame at the nominal standing pose. This preserves the CAD assembly, including the unusually oriented right rear thigh and shank. The new kinematic chains share +X hip abduction and +Y thigh/calf pitch. Each hip and thigh joint center is taken from the source assembled URDF; the thigh joint is offset 16.71 mm fore/aft, 16.71 mm laterally, and 23.09 mm downward from the hip. The effective thigh is 0.12 m and the knee-to-foot planar distance is 0.139291 m. The source foot is also about 27 mm inward of the knee; the generated model uses a mirrored mean of 27.151 mm (0.35 mm discrepancy per side) so motion mirroring remains exact. The fitted calf collision cylinders are transformed through the same chain as the meshes. `configs/robots/dog.yaml` checks the resulting kinematic layout. The output motion library must be rebuilt if these dimensions change.

The original `base_link` mesh is a CAD marker absent on the physical robot, so both its mesh and 0.0014068 kg mass are omitted. Its frame is replaced by a single trunk link based on `main_body_1` (0.757692 kg). Leg masses are preserved from the URDF: 0.1065874 kg hip, 0.068327 kg thigh, 0.1292148 kg shank, and 0.01 kg fixed foot. The last two are merged into a 0.1392148 kg calf; total model mass is 2.0142088 kg. Each calf's centre of mass and full inertia tensor are transformed from the source shank and fixed-foot inertials and combined with the parallel-axis theorem. In the corrected calf frames, the centre of mass is about 6.7 mm forward, 26.8 mm inward, and 58.9 mm below the knee joint. The other link inertias are positive-definite, diagonal estimates from approximate dimensions. All CAD inertial values remain **simulation estimates**, not measured inertias. Re-measure mass, centre of mass and inertia before deploying a real robot policy.

The source ROS2 controller specifies 2.2 N m continuous torque, 3.578102027027027 N m stall torque, 4.717106086471161 rad/s no-load speed, 0.01 kg m² joint armature, and 40/1 PD gains. The MJCF uses 0.01 armature, 0.02 passive damping and 2.2 N m PD effort limit. The current mjlab builtin position actuator has a constant effort limit; it does not reproduce the source controller's torque-speed envelope. The original 3.578 N m URDF effort corresponds to stall torque and is unsuitable as a continuous simulation limit. The corrected URDF records 2.2 N m effort and no-load speed.

The proximal thigh collision capsules meet the trunk shell at the hip cutouts; the MJCF excludes trunk–thigh contacts for these four assembled pairs. Without this exclusion, the first rough model had four 14 mm self-penetrations. The companion URDF retains its collision shapes; simulator-specific self-collision filtering must be configured by a URDF consumer.

The joint ranges are symmetric, kinematic simulation ranges chosen to permit the knee-backward retargeting branch. They are not certified hardware stops. Nominal standing height is 0.22357 m. Home thigh/calf angles are taken from the assembled CAD joint centers and foot positions for each leg; see `dog.py`. World-model scales in `dog.py` are initial height-scaled estimates; run `scripts/calibrate_scales.py` after building a dog motion library.
